NEXO Speaker System Design

Utilizing NEXO’s NS-1 sound system simulation software, designed and proposed a loudspeaker system.

Overview

This was a project, where we were given a hypothetical senario and a hypothetical budget of $80000 to equip a given theater hall with an appropriate and performing loudspeaker system.

Process

This project required us to select and to design the system, considering where to best position the speaker arryas or subs, so as to hit required SPL levels at varying considtions. We had to utilise the NEXO NS-1 simultaion app to simulate our system designs and present a proof of concept and design.

Outcome

Within the class, my team was awareded the best system design for this project, providing the most cohesive and well thought through system amongst the rest. Our design was able to meet the minimum requirements, and also beyond, ensuring headroom for the system to perform at.
