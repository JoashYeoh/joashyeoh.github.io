Pseudo Podcast Audio Editing Project

Utilizing Adobe Audition tools to edit self recorded podcast audio files.

Show Highlights

Overview

This was a podcast project where each individual in the team has to be incharge of editing the audio track of another team memeber's speech recording in the podcast. We also had to draft out and prepare a topic to talk about.

Process

After recoding the tracks via a MixPre-6 II, we edited our respective tracks on Adobe Audition, using paramatric filters, dynamic compressors, deessers, spectral editing and more. After each individual's tracks were done, I mastered the whole mix and added some other music to come in at certain parts of the podcast.

Outcome

We got to learn how to apply the various filters, and what they do. Not only that, through the process of preparing, we also got to learn more about the topic that we talked about.
