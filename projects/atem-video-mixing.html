Multi-Cam / Multi-Source Video Mixing

Media graphics and video switching operator in Church. Working with ATEM video switchers and more.

Overview

I volunteer in the media crew for my Church, and in one of the roles that I play, I primarity run the graphics (lyrics, graphics, slides, etc) but also am involved in some minor video mixing as well. The above instance is for a children's Sunday School hence a simpler set up with one cam and one graphics source.

Process

Typically for graphics, we would utilise ProPresenter as the presentation software, while utilising SDI ATEM Switchers for video mixing/switching. Typically we mix only if there is some form of broadcast/refording, else often it is just graphics.

Outcome

Personally through these experinces, I get to explore abit more on the various tech utilised. Since it is a relatively low stake situation in relation to other productions that my Church does, it allows space for me to thinker around and to learn the ropes of operating the system on my own. (which for the most part I somewhat did)
